History

Rockstar Games is an American video game publisher founded in 1998 by Sam Houser, Dan Houser, Terry Donovan, Jamie King, and Gary Foreman. The company is best known for its successful "Grand Theft Auto" series, which began in 1997 under the development of DMA Design, later renamed Rockstar North. Over the years, Rockstar Games has expanded its portfolio with franchises such as "Red Dead", "Max Payne", and "Midnight Club".

In 2014, Rockstar Games was honored with a BAFTA Academy Fellowship Award for its outstanding contribution to the video game industry. That same year, founders Sam and Dan Houser were inducted into the Hall of Fame of the Academy of Interactive Arts and Sciences. In 2020, Dan Houser left the company to pursue other projects, leaving Sam Houser in charge of the company.

Rockstar Games has consistently pushed the boundaries of video game design, creating open-world experiences that are both immersive and innovative. Titles like "Grand Theft Auto V" and "Red Dead Redemption 2" are celebrated for their expansive worlds, intricate narratives, and attention to detail, setting new standards for storytelling in gaming. These games also introduced cutting-edge technology in terms of graphics, AI, and physics, making them critical milestones in the industry.

Milestones

  • Release of the first "Grand Theft Auto" in 1997, marking the beginning of an iconic franchise.
  • Launch of "Grand Theft Auto III" in 2001, introducing a 3D perspective and open-world, revolutionizing the genre.
  • Release of "Red Dead Redemption" in 2010, a critically acclaimed game set in the American West.
  • Launch of "Grand Theft Auto V" in 2013, becoming the second best-selling game of all time with over 200 million copies sold.
  • Release of "Red Dead Redemption 2" in 2018, praised for its narrative depth and detailed world.

Current Projects

Although Rockstar Games has not officially announced new projects, persistent rumors suggest the development of "Grand Theft Auto V". Fans also speculate about potential remasters of classic titles for next-gen consoles.

Description

"Grand Theft Auto V" follows the story of three criminals in the fictional city of Los Santos, allowing players to switch between protagonists. The game is known for its expansive and detailed open world, as well as its multiplayer mode, "GTA Online".

Awards

  • Game of the Year by multiple publications, including GameSpot and IGN in 2013.
  • BAFTA Games Award for Best British Game in 2014.
