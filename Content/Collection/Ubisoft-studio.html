History

Ubisoft, founded in 1986 by the Guillemot brothers, began as a small distributor before becoming a global leader in game development. Known for iconic franchises like "Assassin's Creed," "Far Cry," and "Rainbow Six," the company has consistently pushed the boundaries of open-world gaming and immersive storytelling.

With studios worldwide, Ubisoft has pioneered innovative mechanics, such as the parkour system in "Assassin's Creed" and the "Play as Anyone" feature in "Watch Dogs: Legion." Their games often blend historical settings with modern gameplay, creating memorable experiences for players.

The 2007 launch of "Assassin's Creed" was a milestone, introducing a revolutionary open-world experience with historical accuracy and free-flowing parkour mechanics. This franchise became a defining pillar for Ubisoft, spawning multiple sequels and spin-offs.

Throughout the 2010s, Ubisoft continued to innovate with major releases such as "Far Cry 3," "Watch Dogs," and "The Division." The company also embraced online multiplayer experiences, launching games like "Rainbow Six Siege," which remains a popular tactical shooter with a strong esports presence.

Ubisoft has faced challenges, including industry-wide shifts towards live-service games and controversies over workplace culture. However, the company remains a dominant force in the gaming world, investing in cutting-edge technologies like AI-driven game design and cloud gaming.

Milestones

  • Release of "Assassin's Creed" in 2007.
  • Launch of "Far Cry 3" in 2012.
  • Release of "Watch Dogs" in 2014.
  • Launch of "Assassin's Creed Valhalla" in 2020.
  • Release of "Far Cry 6" in 2021.

Awards

  • BAFTA Games Award for Best Game (Assassin's Creed) in 2007.
  • The Game Awards - Best Action/Adventure (Watch Dogs) in 2014.

Current Projects

Ubisoft is currently working on new entries in the "Assassin's Creed" and "Far Cry" series, alongside remasters of classic titles. The company is also investing in live-service games and exploring emerging technologies like cloud gaming and AI.
